New article: ‘AI is like fire’

A new article on language teachers’ attitudes towards artificial intelligence has been published in Journal of Education for Teaching: International Research and Pedagogy.

Publication page Read via DOI Back to news

Publication announcement

Published online on 19 May 2026.

Alba Paz-López, Liudmila Shafirova and Boris Vázquez-Calvo’s article, ‘AI is like fire’: a discourse analysis of language teachers’ attitudes towards artificial intelligence, is published in Journal of Education for Teaching.

The article examines language teachers’ attitudes towards AI through discourse analysis. The publication page includes the complete citation and official access links.
