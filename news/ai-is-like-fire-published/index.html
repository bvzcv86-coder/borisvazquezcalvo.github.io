New article: ‘AI is like fire’

A new article on language teachers’ attitudes towards artificial intelligence has been published in Journal of Education for Teaching: International Research and Pedagogy.

Open publication page Read via DOI

I am pleased to share the publication of ‘AI is like fire’: A discourse analysis of language teachers’ attitudes towards artificial intelligence, co-authored with Alba Paz-López and Liudmila Shafirova.

The article examines how language teachers discursively make sense of artificial intelligence, focusing on the metaphors, evaluations, expectations and concerns through which AI is imagined in language education.

The title metaphor, AI is like fire, captures the ambivalence that many educators experience: AI can be productive, useful and transformative, but it also requires care, critical judgment, ethical awareness and pedagogical responsibility.

Why it matters

As generative AI becomes increasingly embedded in educational settings, teacher perspectives are essential. This article contributes to debates on AI literacy, language teacher education, teacher attitudes, discourse analysis and the changing role of language teachers in technology-rich environments.

Full-text access is currently available through the publisher. The PDF is not hosted on this website while journal access and embargo conditions apply.
