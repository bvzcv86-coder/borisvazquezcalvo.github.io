Projects

My research projects examine how digital technologies, online communities, participatory cultures and artificial intelligence reshape language learning, literacy practices, teacher education and learner agency.

Current agenda

My current work focuses on AI-mediated literacies, digitally mediated languaging, learner agency, language teacher education and the social life of language learning online.

Emerging research agenda

AIM

AIM develops a research agenda on AI-mediated literacies and languaging, focusing on how young people and future language educators interact with generative AI, interpret algorithmic feedback, imagine language learning and negotiate agency, authorship and identity.

Funded projects

Selected competitive and institutional research projects that structure my work on digital language learning, participatory cultures and language teacher education.

PI · National project

DEFINERS

Digital Language Learning of Junior Language Teachers examined how junior and preservice language teachers engage with digital technologies, online platforms, AI-powered tools and informal learning environments as part of their professional development.

Role: Principal Investigator
Funding: Spanish national research funding and European Union NextGenerationEU/PRTR support
Core areas: digital language learning, teacher education, informal learning, AI, autonomy and digital literacies
PI · Research project

SEGUE

Social Media and Video Games for Language Learning and Teaching explored how social media, gaming environments and participatory cultures foster language development, literacy practices and pedagogical reflection.

Role: Principal Investigator
Funding: University of Málaga Research Plan
Core areas: social media, video games, language learning, digital literacies and teacher education
Postdoctoral project

Gaming as Literacy

Gaming as Literacy investigated video games as academic and vernacular literacy practices, paying attention to how gaming communities support informal learning, digital communication and language development.

Role: Postdoctoral researcher
Funding: Xunta de Galicia postdoctoral funding
Core areas: video games, literacy studies, digital cultures, informal language learning and online communities
Research line

Digital language education and public scholarship

Across projects, I develop open materials, teacher education workshops, public-facing writing and research-based resources that connect academic work with educational practice and social debate.

Outputs: open educational resources, workshops, public scholarship, conference presentations and teaching materials
Core areas: knowledge transfer, open science, teacher education and digital literacy pedagogy

Cross-project themes

These projects are connected by a common interest in how language learning is shaped by digital participation, social interaction, multimodality, platforms and educational change.

Digitally mediated language learning How learners and teachers use platforms, online communities and intelligent systems to participate in language learning practices.
Digital literacies and participatory cultures How memes, fanfiction, fansubbing, video games, social media and online affinity spaces reshape language and literacy education.
Language teacher education How future and early-career teachers interpret, appropriate and critically evaluate digital technologies and AI in their professional development.
AI, agency and algorithmic awareness How learners and teachers make sense of generative AI, feedback, authorship, evaluation, automation and algorithmic mediation.
