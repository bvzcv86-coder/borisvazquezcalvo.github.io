Ramón y Cajal Researcher · University of Seville

Boris Vázquez-Calvo

I study how people learn and teach languages through the things they read, translate, and create online.

My research follows learners from fan translation communities to teacher education classrooms. I’m currently interested in how future language teachers judge the texts, tools, and AI-generated feedback they use in their work.

Academic profile

Research leadership

I led DEFINERS, a €72,500 national project on the digital learning practices of junior and preservice language teachers, and SEGUE, a University of Málaga project on social media and games.

Project funding and outputs

Editorial work

I have served as Associate Editor of Language Learning & Technology since July 2025. The journal publishes research on how technology shapes language learning and teaching.

Teaching and supervision

Teacher education · 2025

Memes and identity in language teacher education

What can a meme reveal about how a future teacher understands the profession? This study uses multimodal composing to explore professional identity.

Informal learning · 2023

Informal digital learning of English

Our scoping review maps how researchers have studied learners’ engagement with English in everyday digital settings and identifies questions for future research.

Fan translation · 2021

Guerrilla fan translation in a Catalan-speaking community of gamers

When fans translate a game together, disagreements about a word can become opportunities to discuss how Catalan works and who the translation is for.
