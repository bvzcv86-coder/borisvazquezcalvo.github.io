About

I work at the intersection of Language Education, Language Didactics, applied linguistics, digital literacies, online discourse, informal learning, fandom, AI, TELL/CALL and language teacher education.

I am a Ramón y Cajal Researcher in Language Education at the University of Seville. My work examines how contemporary communicative practices — from fan translation, memes, video games and social media to generative AI and algorithmic platforms — transform language learning, teaching, literacy practices and teacher identities.

Across my research, I treat digital environments not merely as tools, but as social, cultural and semiotic ecologies where people participate, create, affiliate, learn languages and make meaning. This has led me to study digital wilds, fandom, participatory culture, online discourse, informal learning, teacher education, TELL/CALL and the pedagogical possibilities of contemporary literacies.

Research areas

I approach language learning as a socially situated, digitally mediated and culturally embedded practice.

Digital mediation

Digitally mediated language learning and literacies

Platforms, online communities, social media, video games, fandoms and intelligent technologies as environments for language learning, literacy development and participation.

Informal learning

Informal and self-directed language learning

Learners’ engagement with languages beyond the classroom, especially across digital wilds, interest-driven communities and platform-based ecologies.

Fandom

Fan translation and participatory cultures

Fansubbing, fandubbing, fanfiction, scanlation, memes, video games and other participatory practices as spaces for language learning and identity work.

TELL/CALL

Technology-enhanced and computer-assisted language learning

Research on how digital tools, platforms, AI, mobile media, online interaction and multimodal resources mediate language learning, teaching, feedback, assessment and learner agency.

Online discourse

Online discourse, identity and digital participation

How learners and teachers construct identities, affiliations and trajectories through language, technology, platforms, multimodal communication and online discourse practices.

AI within digital ecologies

AI, algorithms and language education

Artificial intelligence and algorithmic systems as part of broader digitally mediated environments that reshape authorship, feedback, assessment, critical literacy and teacher education.
