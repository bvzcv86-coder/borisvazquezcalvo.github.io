Catalan teenagers’ identity, literacy and language practices on YouTube

Boris Vazquez-Calvo, Nikolaj Elf, Adriana Gewerc

Technology and the Psychology of Second Language Learners and Users · 2020

A study of how Catalan teenage gamers present themselves on YouTube through language, images and video-related literacy practices.

Approach

Digital ethnography with three teenage gamers, drawing on interviews, screenshots and video analysis through New Literacy Studies.

Main ideas

  • The cases show different degrees of participation and specialisation on YouTube.
  • Choices of usernames, images and language reveal varying linguistic and multimodal awareness across Catalan, Spanish and English.

Three situated qualitative cases of teenage gamers.

How this work can be used

Use the chapter in research on youth digital identities and multilingual media practices, or to inform reflection on self-presentation and audience in student video projects.
