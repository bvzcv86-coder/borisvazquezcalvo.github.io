Journal article · 2023 · Informal learning and social media

The emerging phenomenon of L2 vlogging on Bilibili: characteristics, engagement, and informal language learning

Leticia-Tian Zhang, Boris Vázquez-Calvo, Daniel Cassany

Profesional de la información · 2023

A study of Chinese learners’ Spanish-language vlogs on Bilibili, examining video production, multilingual writing and interaction around learning.

Study and methods

Qualitative virtual ethnography and analysis of 134 Chinese-produced Spanish-language vlogs.

Key findings

  • Vloggers combine resources from formal education and informal media practices.
  • Subtitles, translation, feedback requests and danmu comments support multimodal expression and reflection on language.

The findings describe practices on one platform and in a particular learner context.

How this work can be used

Use this study to inform research on learner-created video and to consider multilingual subtitles, reflection and peer feedback in language-learning projects.
