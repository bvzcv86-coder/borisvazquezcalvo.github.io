Journal article · 2021 · Fan translation and gaming

Guerrilla fan translation, language learning, and metalinguistic discussion in a Catalan-speaking community of gamers

Boris Vazquez-Calvo

ReCALL · 2021

A study of a Catalan-speaking gaming community translating games from English into Catalan, with attention to collaboration and discussion about language.

Study and methods

Digital ethnography with online interviews and observation; content analysis and focused analysis of metalinguistic discussions.

Key findings

  • The translators develop shared roles and a community identity connected with producing cultural content in Catalan.
  • Language learning opportunities arise through translating, discussing doubts with peers and reviewing prospective members’ translation tests.

Qualitative evidence from one fan-translation community.

How this work can be used

Use this study for research on collaborative translation, language learning in digital wilds and the role of metalinguistic discussion in translation-based teaching.
