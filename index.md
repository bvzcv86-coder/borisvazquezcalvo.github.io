# Boris Vázquez-Calvo

**Ramón y Cajal Researcher in Language Education**  
**University of Seville**

## Language learning across digital platforms, participatory cultures, and intelligent technologies.

I study how languages are learned, taught, and lived across digitally mediated environments, including social media, fandoms, video games, online communities, and artificial intelligence.

My research connects language education, applied linguistics, digital literacies, informal language learning, fandom, participatory cultures, AI, and language teacher education.

## Research areas

### Digitally mediated language learning and literacies

I examine how digital platforms, online communities, social media, video games, fandoms, and intelligent technologies shape language learning, literacy practices, and participation.

### Informal and self-directed language learning

I study how learners engage with languages beyond the classroom, especially in digital wilds, platform-based environments, and interest-driven communities.

### Fandom, fan translation, and participatory cultures

My work explores fansubbing, fandubbing, fanfiction, memes, video games, translation fan practices, and participatory cultures as spaces for language learning and identity work.

### AI, algorithms, and language education

I investigate how artificial intelligence and algorithmic systems are reshaping language learning, teaching, assessment, authorship, feedback, and teacher education.

### Language teacher education and pedagogical innovation

I design and study pedagogical approaches that connect contemporary digital practices with language teacher education, including memes, fan-based learning, digital storytelling, and AI-supported tasks.
